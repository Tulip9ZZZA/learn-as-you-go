# Brand Guidelines: Learn As You Go

## 1. Logo Variants & Files

All production vector masters and raster exports live in `assets/` and `brand/assets/`:

- `assets/logo.svg` / `assets/logo.png`: Primary dark-tile horizontal lockup (960x256).
- `assets/logo-transparent.svg` / `assets/logo-transparent.png`: Transparent horizontal lockup for light surfaces.
- `assets/logo-mark.svg` / `assets/logo-mark.png`: Standalone 256x256 symbol mark on Obsidian Slate (`#0B0F17`).
- `assets/logo-symbol-transparent.svg`: Standalone transparent symbol mark.
- `assets/logo-mono-black.svg` / `assets/logo-mono-white.svg`: Single-color monochrome marks for embossing, print, and high-contrast contexts.
- `assets/favicon.svg` / `assets/favicon.png` / `assets/favicon.ico`: Small-size cut tuned for 16px-64px browser tabs.
- `assets/concept-sheet.png`: Phase 6 Concept Overview Sheet comparing Concepts A, B, and C across the 256px/64px/32px/16px scale ladder.

## 2. Clear Space & Minimum Size

- **Clear space**: Maintain at least `1/4` of the symbol height on all sides around the mark.
- **Minimum size**: `16px` height for the simplified symbol (`favicon.svg`); `28px` height for the horizontal lockup.

## 3. Misuse Rules (What Never To Do)

- Do not rotate or mirror the Loopback Detour symbol (the main spine must always point upward/forward on the left).
- Do not add drop shadows, outer glows, or multi-stop decorative gradients to the symbol.
- Do not replace the pure-path vector wordmark with unoutlined system `<text>` tags inside `logo.svg`.
- Do not use more than two accent colors (Return Emerald `#10B981` and Signal Amber `#F59E0B`) on the symbol.
